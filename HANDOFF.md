# R6 PIXEL SIEGE - HANDOFF (checkpoint 2)

Deliverable: `r6-pixel-siege.html` (single self-contained file, no build step, open in a browser).
Published copy: https://claude.ai/artifact/AsaLKcDU5yZAjLrua4DejX
Also included: `ROSTER.md` (research on all 78 operators and the mechanics each needs).

## What is in the build
- Modes: Bomb (sites A/B), Secure Area, Hostage. Best of 3 rounds.
- Side (attack/defend) is assigned at random when you go to operator selection. You cannot choose.
- 60 s loadout timer on the operator screen (auto-deploys at 0). Primary, secondary weapon and secondary gadget are chosen there. No appearance customisation.
- Loading screen (map name, tip, progress bar), then 30 s prep: attackers drive a drone (it can fly through exterior soft-wall windows and shows defenders in line of sight); defender bots reinforce walls one at a time over the 30 s.
- 12 maps on an 80x50 tile grid, generated from data (`MAPS` array + `setMap()`): Building, Embassy, House, Night Club, Bank, Consulate, Hotel, Factory, Hospital, Chalet, Refinery, Skyline. Map picker (or Random) on the main menu. 11+ breakable entry points on the outer wall.
- 8 defender cameras mounted on walls beside doorways. Defender player presses C to cycle/exit. Attackers destroy them by shooting.
- 78 operators (39 attackers, 39 defenders) as data (`NEWOPS` in the data section). 9 primary weapons, 3 secondaries.
- Menus restyled flat, with fade/rise animations. Siege-style loading screen.

## NOT DONE / NOT VERIFIED (do not claim these work)
- Not played by hand. Control feel, bot pacing, balance and difficulty are untested by a human.
- Most operator abilities are STAND-INS: each new operator is mapped to the nearest existing gadget (hammer, ash, thermite, flash, emp, smoke, trap, mat, castle, pulse, doc) or has no active gadget. Real unique mechanics (drones, mirrors, gates, barriers, hacks, rappel, etc.) are not built. See ROSTER.md for the 12 systems needed.
- Operator armour/speed tiers and weapon assignments are from recall plus a few web sources, not verified for all 78. Weapons are generic archetypes, not real gun names.
- Maps are layout variants of one room-grid system with a tint, not hand-built distinct levels. Same outer shell and spawn on every map. Not balanced per map.
- Cameras: defender cameras are 360-degree views, defender bots do not use them, no attacker-side cameras beyond the prep drone.
- No online play. Not possible in a single-file page without a server; needs a relay (e.g. Cloudflare Durable Objects) and host-authoritative sync.
- Reinforcements are still 6 per team. No side swap between rounds.
- Silkscreen font only loads online. Desktop mouse/keyboard only. Not tested in Safari or Firefox.

## Checkpoint 4 (big maps, two floors, pacing, bot setup)
- All 12 maps scaled to roughly 95-113 x 54-64 interior tiles (was 68 x 44); extra rooms generated by repeating the room widths/heights in `grow()`.
- Two floors: the upper floor is stacked in the same grid at row offset `OY`, joined by 2 stair pairs (tile 8, `STAIRS`, `STP`). Stepping on a stair teleports to its partner (`stairs(e)`); pathfinding `field()` treats stairs as links, so bots climb. Bomb site B and the hostage are always upstairs; site A, secure room, extraction and the street are on the ground floor. The upper floor has no exterior entries.
- Minimap shows the floor you are on plus stair markers.
- Pacing: move speed x`PACE` (.85), prep 45 s, action 4:00, `REINF`=9 reinforcements.
- Bots: attackers pick one site as a team and stagger their push (2-7 s delays); defenders reinforce the walls closest to the objective, trap the doors nearest it, face the nearest door during prep, split into anchors and roamers, and roamers investigate a shared alert when any defender sees an attacker.
- Verified headless: all 12 maps load with no JS errors; attacker spawn reaches both sites and hostage; defenders spawn on floor tiles; bots climb and plant upstairs on Building. NOT verified by hand: feel, balance, bot difficulty.
- Not built: rappelling/vaulting, ceiling/floor breaching, upper-floor windows, distinct hand-built layouts per map.
